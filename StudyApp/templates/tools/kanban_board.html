Kanban Board — Guide

The Kanban Board helps you organize your tasks visually. It’s built around three simple stages: To Do, In Progress, and Done. Move tasks between these columns to track your progress with clarity.

How to use:
1. Click + Add Task to create a new task.
2. Drag and drop tasks between columns to update their status.
3. Click the x button to delete a task.
4. Your tasks are saved automatically when you edit or move them.

Use this board to plan your day, manage projects, or track study goals. Keeping tasks visible helps you stay organized and focused.
