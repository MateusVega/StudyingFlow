Pomodoro Timer — Guide

The Pomodoro Timer helps you study with focus and balance. The method is simple: work in short, concentrated sessions, then take a break. It boosts productivity and prevents mental fatigue.

How to use:
1. Choose a mode — Pomodoro (25 min), Short Break (5 min), or Long Break (15 min).
2. Click Start to begin.
3. When the alarm rings, move on to the next cycle.
4. Use Pause to stop and Reset to restart.

After four focus cycles, take a long break.
Stay consistent — that’s what brings real results.

You control the time. The Pomodoro just guides you.
